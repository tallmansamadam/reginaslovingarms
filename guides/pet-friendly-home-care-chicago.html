Pet-Friendly Home Care in Chicago: Keeping Seniors and Their Pets Together

By Samuel Tallman, Chief Brand Officer Published 2026-10-17 Reviewed by: CONFIRM

A dog or cat is often the biggest reason an older adult resists a move. The good news is that many seniors can stay home with a pet if the right help is in place.

What pet care assistance can include

At Regina's Loving Arms, caregivers can help with feeding, walking, litter and cage cleanup, and trips to the vet. This is support for the household's pet, not veterinary care.

Why it matters

  • Routine. Feeding and walking give the day structure.
  • Companionship and activity. Pets encourage movement and engagement.
  • Staying home. A pet is a strong reason families choose in-home care over a move.

Questions to ask any agency

  1. Do caregivers help with pets, and which tasks exactly?
  2. Are caregivers comfortable with the type of animal (large dogs, multiple cats, birds)?
  3. What if the pet needs urgent vet care when the caregiver is on shift?
  4. Is there an extra charge?

Safety and planning

Tell the agency about a pet's behavior, allergies among caregivers, and vet contacts before the first visit. Keep leashes, food, and emergency vet information easy to find.
