Regina's Loving Arms vs. National Home Care Franchises in Chicago: An Honest Comparison

By Samuel Tallman, Chief Brand Officer Published 2026-10-17 Reviewed by: CONFIRM

We're a local agency, so you should expect us to say we're a good choice. Instead, here's a plain comparison so you can decide what fits your family, including where a national brand may be the better fit.

"National franchises" here means brands such as Comfort Keepers, Home Instead, and Right at Home. Franchise offices are locally owned, so quality and pricing vary office to office. We don't make claims about any specific competitor's quality.

Where a national franchise may suit you better

  • You expect to move a parent to another state and want the same brand there.
  • You want a large company's standardized training curriculum and brand recognition.
  • Your family is already comfortable with a specific brand.

Where a local agency may suit you better

  • You want to reach decision-makers directly rather than a regional intake process.
  • You're using insurance or a state program and want someone to verify coverage personally.
  • A pet is part of the household and part of the reason your loved one stays active.
  • You value consistent caregiver matching and a small-agency feel. CONFIRM: add staffing numbers or retention figures only if documented.

How to run this comparison yourself

Ask each agency, local or national, the same five questions: license number, employee vs. contractor status, written pricing, backup plan when a caregiver is out, and which payers they accept. Compare the written answers.

FAQ

Is a national brand safer? A brand name doesn't replace verification. Each office needs its own Illinois license, and you can ask for the number.

Does Regina's Loving Arms cost less? CONFIRM before answering. If rates are lower, state the rate and the comparison basis. If not, say: "We don't claim to be the cheapest. We publish clear rates and verify insurance so you know the real cost."
